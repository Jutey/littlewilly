About

Little Willy (William Crume) is an independent singer-songwriter from Southern California. He put out his first song, “Delivered Nonsense,” in December 2022, and has kept releasing ever since: “Isolated Sadness,” “lmk if you can,” “drive me off the edge,” and the 2026 single “i swear i knew your name.”

Now producing his own beats in FL Studio, Little Willy is chasing a melodic, dreamy sound: heavy bass, space to breathe, and songs that hit harder in the quiet parts.
